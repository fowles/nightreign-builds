# Ironeye

> Researched 2026-10-30. Sources at bottom. Game patch at time of research: App 1.03.5 (hotfix, 2026-03-31); last balance patch 1.03.2 / Regulation 1.03.4 (2026-01-15). A PS4/PS5-only security hotfix (1.23 / 1.003.004, 2026-09-18) was reported with no balance changes.
> Anything marked **(unverified)** came from a single source or conflicting sources.

## Kit

| | |
|---|---|
| Base stat scaling | Dex A, Arc B, Endurance B, Vigor C, Mind C, Str C, Int D, Fai D (wiki.gg and Fextralife agree). HP 820 at Lv 15. |
| Starting weapon | Ironeye's Bow |
| Passive | **Eagle Eye**: +30 hidden Discovery (better drops, chests and boss rewards). Shared with allies. |
| Character Skill | **Marking**: i-frame dash (17 frames) that applies a weak point to enemies it passes through. Marked enemies take +10% damage from all sources. The weak point lasts 17.5 s and is extended by hitting it. Enough damage on the mark makes it burst for big damage and stagger. Cooldown 10 s (wiki.gg). 1 charge base. |
| Ultimate Art | **Single Shot**: greatbow arrow that ignores guard, pierces enemies and terrain, and leaves an AoE shockwave. Charging gives i-frames and aim but **no extra damage**. Can revive a teammate with 2 or fewer near-death bars. |
| Stats that matter | Dexterity, Arcane (bleed/poison weapons), Vigor (low HP pool) |
| Stats that don't (near-free curses) | Strength (C), Intelligence (D), Faith (D) |

"Skill" = Marking. "Ult/Art" = Single Shot.

## Playstyles / archetypes

| Archetype | Weapons | Loop | Wants |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Poison bow** (most cited) | Bows with poison (starting bow + poison relic) | Arrows build poison → Marking on a poisoned target does heavy poison damage (deep) → charged Single Shot adds 300 poison | [Ironeye] deep heavy poison, Art Charge poison, Starting armament inflicts poison, Attack power up when facing poison-afflicted enemy, +1 skill use, cooldown reduction |
| **Mark / crit bow DPS** | Bow or greatbow (Lion Greatbow cited), melee backup | Keep the weak point up for +10% team damage, pop marks, run Art often | Extends duration of weak point, +1 skill use, Improved Bow Attack Power (nerfed 1.03.2), Critical hits fill more of the Art gauge |
| **Thrust-counter hybrid** (niche) | Bow + thrusting melee (spear, rapier, dagger) | After Single Shot, +20% thrusting-counter damage for 30 s | [Ironeye] Boosts thrusting counterattacks after executing Art, HP Restoration upon Thrusting Counterattack. Whether arrows count as thrusting counters is **unknown**. |

## Character-tagged relic effects

Ratings: **Poison** = poison bow, **Mark** = mark/crit DPS, **Thrust** = thrust-counter hybrid.

| Effect | Kind | What it does | Poison | Mark | Thrust | Owned |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Ironeye] +1 additional Character Skill use | std | Stores 2 Markings | core | core | good | 11 |
| [Ironeye] Extends duration of weak point | std | Weak point 17.5 → 22 s per wiki.gg. The extension was raised in 1.02.2, so wiki.gg's number may predate it **(unverified)**. | good | core | good | 15 |
| [Ironeye] Art Charge Activation Adds Poison Effect | std | Fully charged Single Shot: 300 poison on the main arrow (not the AoE) | core | niche | skip | 12 |
| [Ironeye] Boosts thrusting counterattacks after executing Art | std | +20% thrusting-counter damage for 30 s after Single Shot | skip | niche | core | 11 |
| [Ironeye] Character Skill Inflicts Heavy Poison Damage on Poisoned Enemies | deep | Marking adds 70 poison. Against an already-poisoned target it deals 250 + 0.25% max HP. | core | good | niche | 32 |
| [Ironeye] Improved Arcane, Reduced Dexterity | deep | Arc +15, Dex −9 | good (Arc boosts poison/bleed buildup on Arcane-scaling weapons) | skip | skip | 6 deep, 1 std |
| [Ironeye] Improved Vigor and Strength, Reduced Dexterity | deep | Vigor +5, Str +20, Dex −13 | skip | skip | niche (Str melee hybrid) | 3 deep, 1 std |

**Stat swaps:** Dexterity is his A stat, so both cost real damage on Dex bows. Arcane +15 / Dex −9 is the interesting one for a status-focused build. Vigor/Str only suits a Strength melee off-meta build. The standard copies come from fixed DLC Scene relics (Grand Luminous Scene 3 and Grand Tranquil Scene 3, Collector Signboard after the Dreglord), with magnitudes **(unverified)**.

## Meta notes and patch history

| Patch | Change |
|---|---|
| 1.02 (2025-07-31) | Marking's mark more visible; Partial HP Restoration upon Post-Damage Attacks now applies to bows |
| 1.02.2 (2025-09-10) | "[Ironeye] Extends duration of weak point" extension increased |
| 1.03 / 1.03.1 | Marking weak-point visibility desync fixed; Improved Rolling Attacks now applies to bows; Partial HP Restoration triggers on bow crits; "Improved ●● Attack Power" buff accidentally applied to bows |
| 1.03.2 (2026-01-15) | **Improved Bow Attack Power** and **3+ Bows** effects reduced (bug-fix of the 1.03 accident); no kit changes |
| 1.03.5 (2026-03-31) | Hotfix only |

Consensus: poison bow plus the deep heavy-poison Marking is the most-cited build. Mark uptime gives the team +10% damage. Eagle Eye makes him a welcome co-op pick. Most build guides I found date from 2025, so check them against 1.03.2's bow nerf.
