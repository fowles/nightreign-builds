# Recluse

> Researched 2026-10-30. Sources at bottom. Game patch at time of research: App 1.03.5 (multiplayer hotfix, 2026-03-31); last balance patch 1.03.2 / Regulation 1.03.4 (2026-01-15). A PS4/PS5-only update (1.23 / 1.003.004, 2026-09-18) was reported by MP1st with no gameplay changes. No newer balance patch found.
> Anything marked **(unverified)** came from a single source or conflicting sources.

## Kit

| | |
|---|---|
| Base stat scaling | Int S, Faith S, Mind A, End C, Dex C, Arc C, Vigor D, Str D (wiki.gg grades). Lv15: Vig 33, Mind 30, End 23, Str 12, Dex 19, Int 51, Fai 51, Arc 10. HP 740, FP 195, Stamina 94. Long-i-frame dodge roll. |
| Starting weapon | A staff plus a sacred seal. Exact item names weren't confirmed in my sources **(unverified)**. |
| Passive | **Elemental Defense**: affinity damage leaves a colored residue ring on the target (color = last affinity type that hit it). Residue is collectable for FP. Residue from the locked-on target is prioritised; ally residue is used only if no enemy residue is available. |
| Character Skill | **Magic Cocktail**: collect residue (restores FP). The 3rd collection casts a cocktail spell instead, chosen by the 3 colors (Magic/Fire/Lightning/Holy; 14 combos). No cooldown. Notable combos: Magic+Lightning piercing beam, Lightning+Holy auto-parry shield, Fire+Holy orb (ally +30% HP inside), Magic+Holy+Lightning invulnerable ice storm. |
| Ultimate Art | **Soulblood Song**: brands enemies with red sigils. Branded enemies take extra damage, and Nightfarers hitting them restore HP and FP. Deals 40 Revival damage (clears a single-bar near-death gauge). |
| Stats that matter | Int (sorceries), Faith (incantations), Mind/FP, Vigor (lowest-HP tier) |
| Stats that don't (near-free curses) | Strength, Arcane. Dexterity is mostly free, but see the Dex swap below. |

"Skill" = Magic Cocktail (residue collection). "Ult/Art" = Soulblood Song. No [Recluse] effect uses the word "ability". Affinity-residue effects trigger on residue collection (the skill).

## Playstyles / archetypes

| Archetype | Weapons | Loop | Wants |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sorcery caster** (default) | Staff (Carian Regal Scepter is a common top pick) | Spam sorceries, collect residue for FP and Terra Magica, cocktail for burst/defense | Terra Magica, Improved Sorceries, Magic Attack Power Up, Int/Faith swap, FP effects |
| **Dual-school caster** | Staff + seal (Erdtree Seal, Dragon Communion Seal) | Mix magic and fire/holy/lightning to control which cocktail you get | Improved Affinity Attack Power, both "Improved Sorceries" and "Improved Incantations", Int/Faith swap |
| **Ult-buff / survivability** | Either | Pop Soulblood Song for +16% attack, +50% max HP and team sustain | [Recluse] Art buffs, Ultimate Art Auto Charge, Art gauge effects |

## Character-tagged relic effects

Ratings: **Sorc** = sorcery caster, **Dual** = dual-school, **Ult** = ult-buff.

| Effect | Kind | What it does (wiki.gg) | Sorc | Dual | Ult | Owned |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Recluse] Collecting affinity residue activates Terra Magica | std | Terra Magica sigil for 30 s; standing in it gives +15% magic damage | core | good | good | 12 |
| [Recluse] Suffer blood loss and increase attack power upon Art activation | std | +16% attack power for 30 s. Self-inflicted blood loss costs 20% max HP. | good | good | core | 10 |
| [Recluse] Activating Ultimate Art raises Max HP | std | +50% max HP for 30 s. Since 1.02.2 the buff persists past the timer until you take damage. | good | good | core | 6 |
| [Recluse] Collect Affinity Residues to Negate Affinity | deep | +20% negation of the matching affinity for 30 s after collecting residue | good | good | good | 21 |
| [Recluse] Improved Intelligence and Faith, Reduced Mind | deep | Int +12, Faith +12, Mind −13 | core | core | good | 6 deep, 1 std |
| [Recluse] Improved Vigor, Endurance, and Dexterity, Reduced Intelligence and Faith | deep | Vigor +4, End +5, Dex +20, Int −10, Faith −10 | skip | skip | niche | 8 deep, 1 std |

Every known [Recluse] effect is owned. The blood-loss and max-HP Art effects pair well: +50% max HP absorbs the 20% bleed (order of application unverified).

**Stat swaps:**
- **Int/Faith +12, Mind −13**: the caster pick. It boosts both schools; the cost is about 13 Mind of FP pool, which residue collection refunds.
- **Vig/End/Dex up, Int/Faith −10**: wiki.gg frames it as survivability plus faster casting (Dex) at the cost of spell damage. Losing 10 of each casting stat is a big damage cut, so it only suits a safety-first or melee-hybrid setup.
- The standard copies are DLC Scene relics (Grand Drizzly Scene: Vigor +3 / End +3 + swap; Grand Tranquil Scene: Int +3 / Faith +3 + swap). They aren't in my unique-relic list, so query the db for their relic_ids.

## Dormant Powers

| Dormant Power | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Dormant Power Helps Discover Staves | neutral (on-build for sorcery) |
| Dormant Power Helps Discover Sacred Seals | neutral for dual-school; avoid for pure sorcery |
| Any melee weapon type | avoid |

Staves and Seals together on a dual-school build are fine. They're both on-build, so they don't really dilute each other.

## Curse considerations

| Curse | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Reduced Faith and Strength | near-free for pure sorcery, bad for dual | Strength is unused |
| Reduced Strength and Intelligence | bad for sorcery, near-free for a pure-Faith build | |
| Reduced Intelligence and Dexterity / Reduced Dexterity and Faith | bad | Each hits a casting stat |
| Reduced Vigor and Arcane | acceptable-to-bad | Arcane is free, but she's squishy (740 HP) |
| Reduced Damage Negation After Evading / Repeated Evasions Lower Damage Negation | bad | Dodging is her main defense |
| Taking Damage Causes X Buildup | acceptable (ranged, gets hit less); still avoid Death/Sleep | |
| Ultimate Art Charging Impaired | bad with the Art-tagged relics; tolerable for a pure-sorcery setup | |
| Continuous HP Loss / Lower Attack When Below Max HP | bad with the blood-loss Art effect | It keeps you below max HP |
