# Duchess

> Researched 2026-10-30. Sources at bottom. Game patch at time of research: App 1.03.5 (hotfix, 2026-03-31); last balance patch 1.03.2 / Regulation 1.03.4 (2026-01-15). A PS4/PS5-only security hotfix (1.23 / 1.003.004, 2026-09-18) was reported with no balance changes.
> Anything marked **(unverified)** came from a single source or conflicting sources.

## Kit

| | |
|---|---|
| Base stat scaling | Int A, Dex B, Fai B, Mind B, Vigor C, Arc C, Str D, Endurance D (wiki.gg and Fextralife agree). wiki.gg's Lv 15 Dex is 41 in the infobox but 45 in the level table **(unverified)**. |
| Starting weapon | Duchess' Dagger |
| Passive | **Magnificent Poise**: dodge and backstep cost 75% less stamina. Dodge is a short dash that chains into a second dodge (21 i-frames total across both). |
| Character Skill | **Restage**: replays hits that nearby enemies took in the last 3 s at 50% damage, including stance damage and **status buildup**. Usable mid-animation. Cooldown 12 s (wiki.gg). |
| Ultimate Art | **Finale**: Duchess and nearby allies turn invisible, and enemies drop aggro. Duration **15 s per wiki.gg vs ~10 s per Fextralife**. 1.02.2 increased it, so Fextralife is likely stale **(unverified)**. I-frames during the cast only. Attacking doesn't break it, but enemies swing toward you. |
| Stats that matter | Dexterity (daggers), Intelligence (sorcery/magic daggers), Faith (Divine variant), Mind |
| Stats that don't (near-free curses) | Strength, Endurance (stamina is worth 0 anyway); Arcane mostly (C) unless using Arcane bleed daggers |

"Skill" = Restage. "Ult/Art" = Finale.

## Playstyles / archetypes

| Archetype | Weapons | Loop | Wants |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Status dagger + Restage** (most cited) | Bleed/frost daggers (Reduvia, Blood Scavenger's Curved Swords early) | Dump a dagger combo → Restage doubles the bleed/frost buildup and damage. Deep invulnerability on Restage covers you. | Improved Character Skill Attack Power, Character Skill Cooldown Reduction, Starting armament inflicts blood loss / frost, Attack power up when facing frostbite-afflicted enemy, deep i-frames |
| **Dagger chain reprise** | Daggers (Crown Medal) | Finishing a dagger chain triggers a free mini-Restage (36–39%) that ignores cooldown | [Duchess] Reprise events…, Improved Dagger Attack Power, Improved Attack Power with 3+ Daggers Equipped, chain-attack finisher boosts |
| **Stealth / crit assassin** | Daggers (best crit multipliers) | Finale → kill under invisibility for stacking +15% AP. Backstab crits grant 9 s of stealth. | Defeating enemies while Art is active…, Become difficult to spot…, Improved Critical Hits (+1), Critical hits fill more of the Art gauge |
| **Divine / caster Duchess** (niche) | Dagger + seal or staff | Deep Mind+Faith swap (Faith +13) for incantation buffs, or Int-scaling magic | Mind and Faith swap, Faith or Int effects |

## Character-tagged relic effects

Ratings: **Status** = status dagger + Restage, **Chain** = dagger chain reprise, **Stealth** = crit assassin, **Div** = Divine/caster.

| Effect | Kind | What it does | Status | Chain | Stealth | Div | Owned |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Duchess] Improved Character Skill Attack Power | std | Restage replays at 60% instead of 50% | core | good | good | good | 7 |
| [Duchess] Reprise events upon nearby enemies by landing the final blow of a chain attack with dagger | std | Dagger chain finisher triggers a weaker Restage (36–39%) that ignores cooldown. wiki.gg titles it "Successive dagger attack reprises event…", which may be an old name. | good | core | niche | skip | 13 |
| [Duchess] Defeating enemies while Art is active ups attack power | std | +15% damage for 30 s per kill during Finale, stacking | niche | niche | core | niche | 16 |
| [Duchess] Become difficult to spot and silence footsteps after landing critical from behind | std | 9 s of stealth after a backstab | skip | skip | good | skip | 17 |
| [Duchess] Use Character Skill for Brief Invulnerability | deep | Restage grants 0.4 s of invulnerability, usable mid-animation, so it works as a panic button | core | core | core | core | 34 |
| [Duchess] Improved Vigor and Strength, Reduced Mind | deep | Vigor +3, Str +24, Mind −14 | niche (Str weapons only) | skip | skip | skip | 6 deep, 1 std |
| [Duchess] Improved Mind and Faith, Reduced Intelligence | deep | Mind +3, Faith +13, Int −5 | skip | skip | skip | core | 8 deep, 1 std |

**Stat swaps:** Vigor/Str costs 14 Mind for +24 Str, which only matters with Strength-scaling weapons. Not a dagger build. Mind/Faith is cheap (Int −5) and is the core of a Faith Duchess. On a Dex dagger build it's roughly neutral filler. The standard copies come from DLC Scene relics (Grand Burning Scene 3 and Grand Drizzly Scene 1, Collector Signboard after the Dreglord), with magnitudes **(unverified)**.

## Key universal effects

## Meta notes and patch history

| Patch | Change |
|---|---|
| 1.02.1 (2025-08-12) | Finale no longer releases lock-on to eggs or insects attached to the player |
| 1.02.2 (2025-09-10) | Dex level-up gains up; some dagger attacks AP up; two-handed dagger 2nd heavy reduced; **Finale duration increased** |
| 1.03.1 (2025-12-17) | Finale vs. Sentient Pest lose-sight bug fixed |
| 1.03.2 (2026-01-15) | Restage phantom visibility adjusted (cosmetic) |
| 1.03.5 (2026-03-31) | Hotfix only |

Consensus: a high-skill dagger and status Restage character and a strong co-op pick (Finale resets aggro for the team). Vulkk lists the deep Brief Invulnerability and dagger reprise among her Deep-relic effects. I found no Deep-of-Night tier ranking. One 2026 guide calls her best for co-op, but that source describes Restage incorrectly **(low confidence)**.
