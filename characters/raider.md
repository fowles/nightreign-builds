# Raider

> Researched 2026-10-30. Sources at bottom. Game patch at time of research: App 1.03.5 (hotfix, 2026-03-31); last balance patch 1.03.2 / Regulation 1.03.4 (2026-01-15). A PS4/PS5-only security hotfix (1.23 / 1.003.004, 2026-09-18) was reported with no balance changes.
> Anything marked **(unverified)** came from a single source or conflicting sources.

## Kit

| | |
|---|---|
| Base stat scaling | Str S, Vigor A, Endurance A, Dex C, Arc C, Mind D, Int D, Fai D (wiki.gg and Fextralife agree) |
| Starting weapon | Raider's Greataxe |
| Passive | **Fighter's Resolve**: taking damage powers up Retaliate. Can't be knocked down during Retaliate, and lethal damage during it leaves him at 1 HP. Since 1.03.2, a big HP drop grants attack power for a set time. wiki.gg: +50% damage for 10 s at ≤25% HP (exact post-1.03.2 numbers **unverified**). |
| Character Skill | **Retaliate**: hyper-armored stance with increased damage negation, then a punch. After taking ≥850 damage (pre-negation) he steams and the next use is charged: +50% damage, shockwave, Ult-level stagger. Cooldown **not published** by either wiki. Its damage doesn't trigger Partial HP Restoration. |
| Ultimate Art | **Totem Stela**: summons a climbable tombstone for 20 s. Creation deals AoE damage (range widened in 1.03.2), and nearby Nightfarers get +15% physical damage. Blocks arrows and can obstruct allies. I-frames during the wind-up. |
| Stats that matter | Strength, Vigor. Endurance only matters for stamina, which is worth 0 in scoring per CLAUDE.md. |
| Stats that don't (near-free curses) | Intelligence, Faith, Mind (all D); Dexterity and Arcane mostly (C) |

"Skill" = Retaliate. "Ult/Art" = Totem Stela.

## Character-tagged relic effects

Ratings: **Ret** = Retaliate bruiser, **Totem** = Totem support, **Break** = stance-break smasher.

| Effect | Kind | What it does | Ret | Totem | Break | Owned |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Raider] Damage taken while using Character Skill improves attack power and stamina | std | +10% damage and +20% stamina for 20 s after tanking a hit in Retaliate | core | good | good | 13 |
| [Raider] Duration of Ultimate Art extended | std | Totem Stela 20 → 30 s | good | core | niche | 7 |
| [Raider] Hit With Character Skill to Reduce Enemy Attack Power | deep | Enemies hit by Retaliate deal −13% damage for 13 s. 1.03.2 fixed Retaliate sometimes not staggering with this equipped. | core | good | good | 26 |
| [Raider] Improved Mind and Intelligence, Reduced Vigor and Endurance | deep | Mind +9, Int +35, Vigor −8, Endurance −4 | skip | skip | skip | 6 deep, 1 std |
| [Raider] Improved Arcane, Reduced Vigor | deep | Arc +17, Vigor −4 | niche (bleed/rot Arcane weapons, e.g. Rotten Greataxe) | skip | niche | 6 deep, 1 std |

**Stat swaps:** Mind/Int is a gimmick (Int +35 for an Int-scaling colossal weapon or sorcery Raider) and costs Vigor (the Endurance cost is free, since stamina is worth 0). Treat it as negative on normal builds. Arcane/Vigor is cheap (−4 Vigor) and helps only on Arcane-scaling status weapons. The standard copies come from DLC Scene relics (Grand Burning Scene 2 and Grand Tranquil Scene 1, Collector Signboard after the Dreglord), with magnitudes **(unverified)**.

## Meta notes and patch history

| Patch | Change |
|---|---|
| 1.02.2 (2025-09-10) | Greataxe and Great Hammer stagger up; Retaliate attack range up |
| 1.03 (2025-12-03) | Fixed Retaliate cancelling or flinching on some no-damage attacks |
| 1.03.2 (2026-01-15) | **Greataxe, Great Hammer and Colossal attack speed up** (not dual-wield or jump attacks); Fighter's Resolve gains an AP boost on a large HP drop; Totem Stela range up; Totem relic effects (HP on kill, poise) up; Retaliate attack-reduction stagger bug fixed |
| 1.03.5 (2026-03-31) | Hotfix only |

Consensus: 1.03.2 was a clear Raider buff. Guides favor two-handed or dual heavy weapons, Torn Braided Cord, Black Claw Necklace, and "Taking attacks improves attack power". I found no Deep-of-Night-specific Raider guide, and all build guides I found predate or don't cite 1.03.2.
