# Undertaker (Forsaken Hollows DLC)

> Researched 2026-10-30. Sources at bottom. Game patch at time of research: App 1.03.5 (multiplayer hotfix, 2026-03-31); last balance patch 1.03.2 / Regulation 1.03.4 (2026-01-15). A PS4/PS5-only update (1.23 / 1.003.004, 2026-09-18) was reported by MP1st with no gameplay changes. No newer balance patch found.
> Anything marked **(unverified)** came from a single source or conflicting sources.

## Kit

| | |
|---|---|
| Base stat scaling | Str A, Faith A, Vigor B, Mind C, End C, Arc C, Dex D, Int D (wiki.gg). Lv15: Vig 48, Mind 14, End 22, Str 50, Dex 13, Int 5, Fai 41, Arc 10. HP 1040, FP 115, Stamina 92. |
| Starting weapon | Undertaker's Hammer (weapon skill Prayerful Strike: heals self and allies on hit) |
| Passive | **Confluence**: an ally's Ultimate Art, or certain darkness-imbued enemy attacks, grant one bonus Art activation regardless of gauge. Dodging an enemy grab also grants one. Stores 1 max, lasts 3 s. |
| Character Skill | **Trance**: 14 s cooldown, 8 s duration. Refills stamina; free sprinting; dash replaces the roll; 30% damage negation; hyperarmor vs most attacks; +10% damage, rising after a completed light chain. Pressing again cancels and fully resets the cooldown. Empowered version (full Art gauge, Interact + R-heavy + L-heavy): 15 s, auto-dodges all attacks, bigger attack boost, empties the gauge (can spend a Confluence bonus). |
| Ultimate Art | **Loathsome Hex**: flies at the locked target for heavy damage, hitting enemies in the path; invulnerable during it; leaves 25% gauge. Also a gap-closer. |
| Stats that matter | Strength, Faith, Vigor |
| Stats that don't (near-free curses) | Intelligence, Dexterity, Arcane |

"Skill" = Trance. "Ult/Art" = Loathsome Hex. "Executing Art" = casting Loathsome Hex. "Assist effect from incantation" = a self-buff incantation (Golden Vow, Flame, Grant Me Strength, etc.).

## Character-tagged relic effects

Ratings: **Ham** = Str/Faith hammer, **Buff** = incantation-buff, **Stat** = status hammer. Numbers are wiki.gg's. The two 1.03.2-buffed effects may show pre-buff values **(unverified)**.

| Effect | Kind | What it does | Ham | Buff | Stat | Owned |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Undertaker] Attack power increased by landing the final blow of a chain attack | std | +12% damage for 20 s after the last hit of a light-attack chain. Buffed 1.03.2. | core | good | good | 8 |
| [Undertaker] Physical attacks boosted while assist effect from incantation is active for self | std | +15% physical damage for 60 s after a self-buff incantation. Buffed 1.03.2. | niche | core | niche | 18 |
| [Undertaker] Activating Ultimate Art increases attack power | std | +14% damage for 40 s after Loathsome Hex | core | core | good | 7 |
| [Undertaker] Contact with allies restores their HP while Ultimate Art is activated | std | Hex heals each ally it passes through for 30% max HP + 100 | niche | niche | niche | 3 |
| [Undertaker] Executing Art readies Character Skill | deep | Trance is available immediately after Loathsome Hex | core | good | core | 22 |
| [Undertaker] Improved Mind and Faith, Reduced Strength | deep | Mind +9, Faith +12, Strength −15 | skip | good | skip | 3 deep, 1 std |
| [Undertaker] Improved Dexterity, Reduced Vigor and Faith | deep | Dex +19, Vigor −5, Faith −13. wiki.gg: for curved sword/twinblade builds. | skip | skip | skip | 3 deep, 1 std |

Every known [Undertaker] effect is owned. The chain-finisher bonus is "+12%" per wiki.gg. Trance itself also scales its damage bonus on chain completion, but whether the two stack multiplicatively is unknown.

**Stat swaps:**
- **Mind/Faith up, Str −15**: only for a Faith-weighted incantation build. Strength A drives hammer damage, so the meta build skips it.
- **Dex +19, Vigor/Faith down**: off-kit for hammers. wiki.gg suggests it for curved swords/twinblades. Skip.
- The standard copies are DLC Scene relics (Grand Tranquil Scene: Mind +3 / Faith +3 + swap; Grand Drizzly Scene: Dex +3 / Vigor +3 + swap). They aren't in my unique-relic list, so query the db for their relic_ids.

## Curse considerations

| Curse | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Reduced Intelligence and Dexterity | near-free | |
| Reduced Strength and Intelligence / Reduced Faith and Strength | **bad** | Strength A is hammer damage |
| Reduced Dexterity and Faith | bad-ish | Faith A (buffs, Marika's Hammer scales with Faith) |
| Reduced Vigor and Arcane | acceptable | Arcane is free; she has 1040 HP |
| Taking Damage Causes Death / Sleep Buildup | **near-disqualifying** | Frontline hyperarmor brawler; Trance tanks hits instead of dodging |
| Other Taking Damage Causes X Buildup | bad | Same reason; prefer "... When Below Max HP" variants |
| Ultimate Art Charging Impaired | bad | Two core tagged effects trigger on Hex. Confluence bonus activations soften it in co-op only. |
| Reduced Damage Negation After Evading / Repeated Evasions Lower Damage Negation | acceptable | Trance uses a dash and she mostly tanks |
