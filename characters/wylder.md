# Wylder

> Researched 2026-10-30. Sources at bottom. Game patch at time of research: App 1.03.5 (hotfix, 2026-03-31); last balance patch 1.03.2 / Regulation 1.03.4 (2026-01-15). A PS4/PS5-only security hotfix (1.23 / 1.003.004, 2026-09-18) was reported with no balance changes.
> Anything marked **(unverified)** came from a single source or conflicting sources.

## Kit

| | |
|---|---|
| Base stat scaling | Vigor A, Str A, Dex B, Endurance B, Mind C, Int C, Fai C, Arc C. Lv 15: Vig 52 / Str 50 / Dex 40 / End 27 / Mind 19 / Int 15 / Fai 15 / Arc 10 (wiki.gg and Fextralife agree). |
| Starting weapon | Wylder's Greatsword + Wylder's Small Shield |
| Passive | **Sixth Sense**: survive one lethal hit by auto-dodging. Recharges at a Site of Grace or on respawn. Not recharged by a teammate revive. Doesn't trigger on grabs or status damage. |
| Character Skill | **Claw Shot**: grapple that pulls small enemies in, or pulls Wylder to large ones. Jumping at the end gives a long leap. Cooldown **10 s (wiki.gg) vs 8 s (Fextralife) (unverified)**. 1 charge base, 2 with "+1 additional Character Skill use". |
| Ultimate Art | **Onslaught Stake**: explosive stake with i-frames during the wind-up. Uncharged knocks Wylder back and keeps some gauge. Fully charged does more stance damage, no knockback, and drains the full gauge. |
| Stats that matter | Vigor, Strength, Dexterity |
| Stats that don't (near-free curses) | Intelligence, Faith, Arcane (all C, ≤15 at Lv 15) |

"Skill" = Claw Shot. "Ult/Art" = Onslaught Stake. In tagged effect text, **"ability" means the passive, Sixth Sense** (wiki.gg: Silver Tear "greatly fills the Art gauge when Sixth Sense activates").

## Playstyles / archetypes

| Archetype | Weapons | Loop | Wants |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Greatsword fire follow-ups** (signature) | Greatswords (guides cite Ordovis's, Grafted Blade, Blasphemous Blade, Starscourge) | Claw Shot in → light attacks gain +12 fire for 20 s (greatsword only) → Onslaught Stake spreads fire, and the burn now feeds the Art gauge (1.03.2) | +1 skill use, Character Skill Cooldown Reduction, Fire Attack Power Up, Improved Greatsword Attack Power, stamina (the follow-ups cost stamina since 1.02.2) |
| **Bleed Claw Shot** (Deep of Night) | Any. Bleed weapons stack nicely. | Claw Shot applies 60 bleed on contact + 55 on retract. Two charges + cooldown reduction = frequent bleed procs on bosses. | [Wylder] Character Skill inflicts Blood Loss, +1 skill use, cooldown reduction, Starting armament inflicts blood loss |
| **Art spam** | Any | Onslaught Stake i-frames and stagger as often as possible | Ultimate Art Auto Charge, Critical hits / Defeating enemies fills more of the Art gauge, [Wylder] Art activation spreads fire (burn feeds the gauge) |

## Character-tagged relic effects

Ratings: **GS** = greatsword fire, **Bleed** = bleed Claw Shot, **Art** = Art spam.

| Effect | Kind | What it does | GS | Bleed | Art | Owned |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Wylder] +1 additional Character Skill use | std | Stores 2 Claw Shots | core | core | good | 17 |
| [Wylder] Standard attacks enhanced with fiery follow-ups when using Character Skill (greatsword only) | std | Light attacks after Claw Shot add +12 fire damage for 20 s. Consumes stamina since 1.02.2. | core | skip | skip | 16 |
| [Wylder] Art activation spreads fire in area | std | Onslaught Stake leaves burning ground. Burn AP raised in 1.02.2; burn feeds the Art gauge since 1.03.2. | good | niche | core | 13 |
| [Wylder] Art gauge greatly filled when ability is activated | std | Big Art refill when Sixth Sense triggers (once per grace or respawn) | niche | niche | good | 12 |
| [Wylder] Character Skill inflicts Blood Loss | deep | Claw Shot: 60 bleed on contact, 55 on retract | good | core | good | 24 |
| [Wylder] Improved Mind, Reduced Vigor | deep | Mind +10, Vigor −5 | skip | skip | skip | 3 deep, 1 std |
| [Wylder] Improved Intelligence and Faith, Reduced Strength and Dexterity | deep | Int +15, Fai +15, Str −7, Dex −5 | skip | skip | niche | 3 deep, 1 std |

**Stat swaps:** Both trade away his main stats (Vigor or Str/Dex). The Int/Faith swap only fits a caster or incantation Wylder, which isn't a known meta. Mind is near-useless without FP-heavy weapon skills. The standard copies come from fixed DLC Scene relics (Grand Burning Scene 3 and Grand Drizzly Scene 3, Collector Signboard after the Dreglord), and their exact magnitudes are **(unverified)**.

## Curse considerations

| Verdict | Curses (owned text) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Near-free | none fully. Every stat curse in the inventory pairs Str or Dex with something, except Reduced Vigor and Arcane. | Wylder uses Vig/Str/Dex. Exact stat reduction sizes are unknown. |
| Mild | Reduced Intelligence and Dexterity, Reduced Dexterity and Faith (Dex B), Reduced Rune Acquisition | One stat is free |
| Costly | Reduced Strength and Intelligence, Reduced Faith and Strength (Str A), Reduced Vigor and Arcane (Vig A), Reduced Damage Negation After Evading, Repeated Evasions Lower Damage Negation | Wylder dodges a lot |
| Situational | Taking Damage Causes X Buildup, X Buildup When Below Max HP | Wylder doesn't block much, so the chip-damage question matters less than for Guardian. Death and Sleep are still the worst. |
| Disqualifying | Ultimate Art Charging Impaired (for the Art-spam and fire-spread builds) | Kills the 1.03.2 burn-to-gauge loop |

## Meta notes and patch history

| Patch | Change |
|---|---|
| 1.02.2 (2025-09-10) | Claw Shot poise and stamina damage vs. blocking enemies up; greatsword fiery follow-up now **consumes stamina**; Art fire-spread burn AP up, no longer differs one- vs. two-handed |
| 1.03 / 1.03.1 | No Wylder kit changes |
| 1.03.2 (2026-01-15) | "[Wylder] Art activation spreads fire in area": **burn damage now increases the Art gauge** |
| 1.03.5 (2026-03-31) | Hotfix only |

Consensus: a flexible Str/Dex bruiser. Guides recommend greatswords and the Slate Whetstone, Earring and Silver Tear set. One 2026 guide ranks him best for solo **(low-quality source)**. Mobalytics lists the deep Blood Loss Claw Shot as a top character deep effect, but I couldn't fetch the page directly.
