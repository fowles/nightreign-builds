# Guardian

> Researched 2026-10-30. Sources at bottom. Game patch at time of research: App 1.03.5 (hotfix, 2026-03-31); last balance patch 1.03.2 / Regulation 1.03.4 (2026-01-15). A PS4/PS5-only security hotfix (1.23 / 1.003.004, 2026-09-18) was reported by MP1st with no balance changes.
> Anything marked **(unverified)** came from a single source or conflicting sources.

## Kit

| | |
|---|---|
| Base stat scaling | Vigor S, Endurance A, Str B, Dex C, Mind C, Fai C, Arc C, Int D (wiki.gg and Fextralife agree) |
| Starting weapon | Guardian's Halberd + Guardian's Greatshield |
| Passive | **Steel Guard**: hold Guard + tap Dodge to brace. Guard Boost x5, walk speed, slow stamina drain, no dodging. Covers a 180° frontal arc (widened in 1.02.2). Separately, +10% Guard Boost with any shield. |
| Character Skill | **Whirlwind**: 360° spin; charging enlarges the area, pulls enemies in, deflects small projectiles. Cooldown 13 s (wiki.gg; Fextralife doesn't list one). Hits trigger "Stamina restored with each successful attack". |
| Ultimate Art | **Wings of Salvation**: leap + dive with i-frames throughout. Instantly revives downed allies at the landing point. Holding after landing gives allies a damage-immunity aura for up to 5 s or until the gauge runs out (grabs bypass it). Uncharged use costs only half the gauge (Fextralife). |
| Stats that matter | Vigor, Strength (halberd), some Dex. Endurance (guard stamina) is worth 0 in scoring per CLAUDE.md. |
| Stats that don't (near-free curses) | Intelligence; Faith and Arcane (unless running a Faith/incantation variant) |

"Skill" = Whirlwind. "Ult/Art" = Wings of Salvation. In tagged effect text, **"ability" means the passive, Steel Guard** (e.g. "shockwaves while ability is active").

## Playstyles / archetypes

| Archetype | Weapons | Loop | Wants |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Halberd guard-counter tank** (established meta) | Halberd + greatshield | Steel Guard through combos, guard counter on openings, Art for team revive/immunity | Improved Guard Counters, Guard counter is given a boost based on current HP, HP Recovery From Successful Guarding, Successful guarding fills more of the Art gauge, shockwaves, thrusting-counter sustain |
| **Support / Art battery** | Halberd, Commander's Standard is a popular co-op pick **(unverified, single low-quality source)** | Whirlwind for ally damage negation (deep), Art for heals + immunity | [Guardian] Art heal, deep skill negation buff, Art charge effects, Flask Also Heals Allies |
| **Faith Guardian** (niche) | Sacred seal / halberd hybrid | Deep Mind+Faith stat swap (Faith +17) for incantation buffs | Mind and Faith swap, Faith-based effects. Low confidence that this is competitive. |

## Character-tagged relic effects

Ratings: **Tank** = halberd guard-counter, **Sup** = support/Art battery.

| Effect | Kind | What it does | Tank | Sup | Owned |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Guardian] Successful guards send out shockwaves while ability is active | std | Guarding during Steel Guard emits a shockwave (100 base damage per wiki.gg). Power raised in 1.03.2. | core | good | 17 |
| [Guardian] Slowly restores nearby allies' HP while Art is active | std | Heal-over-time aura during Wings of Salvation. wiki.gg gives about 10% max HP + 12 per 0.5 s, but quotes two slightly different formulas **(unverified)**. | good | core | 9 |
| [Guardian] Increased duration for Character Skill | std | Longer fully-charged Whirlwind, which can also deflect larger projectiles | niche | good | 13 |
| [Guardian] Creates whirlwind when charging halberd attacks | std | Fully charged halberd heavies add a small AoE | niche | skip | 12 |
| [Guardian] Character Skill Boosts Damage Negation of Nearby Allies | deep | +12% damage negation for 30 s after Whirlwind (wiki.gg) | good | core | 30 |
| [Guardian] Improved Strength and Dexterity, Reduced Vigor | deep | Str +9, Dex +19, Vigor −8 | niche | skip | 7 deep, 1 std |
| [Guardian] Improved Mind and Faith, Reduced Vigor | deep | Mind +8, Faith +17, Vigor −6 | skip | niche | 8 deep, 1 std |

**Stat swaps:** Both cost Vigor, his S-rank stat and the core of a tank build. Str/Dex fits a damage-leaning halberd build only if you accept less HP. Mind/Faith is only for an incantation variant. The standard-relic copies come from fixed DLC Scene relics (Grand Burning Scene 2 and Grand Luminous Scene 2, Collector Signboard after the Dreglord). wiki.gg lists them alongside "+3" stat lines, so their exact magnitudes are **(unverified)**.

## Key universal effects

| Group | Effects (in-game text) |
|---|---|
| Guard counter offense | **Attack power increased for each Night Invader defeated** (≈+20%, meta-top per CLAUDE.md; standard relics only, 8 owned), Improved Guard Counters (+1/+2; +22%/+26% per aoeah, which predates the 1.03 buff, so likely higher now **(unverified)**), Guard counter is given a boost based on current HP, Improved Halberd Attack Power, Physical Attack Up (+1…+4) |
| Art charge | Successful guarding fills more of the Art gauge (+1), Ultimate Art Auto Charge (+1…+3), Critical hits fill more of the Art gauge |
| Sustain | **Rally** = Partial HP Restoration upon Post-Damage Attacks (non-negotiable for melee per CLAUDE.md), HP Recovery From Successful Guarding (buffed 1.03.2), HP Restoration upon Thrusting Counterattack (+1), Continuous HP Recovery, Increased Maximum HP, Slowly restore HP for self and nearby allies when HP is low |
| Defense / utility | Improved Physical Damage Negation (+1/+2), Improved Affinity Damage Negation, Draw enemy attention while guarding (buffed 1.03.2), Character Skill Cooldown Reduction, Flask Also Heals Allies |
| Stamina | Increased Maximum Stamina: **worth 0 in scoring** (CLAUDE.md: stamina is tie-break only) |

## Dormant Powers

| Status | Dormant Powers |
|---|---|
| On-build (neutral) | Halberds |
| Acceptable alt | Greatshields (shield upgrades raise Guard Boost, and Fextralife says to prioritise them). Great Spears and Spears are thrust weapons that also work with guard counters, but they still dilute halberd drops. |
| Avoid | Everything else (Bows, Ballistas, Crossbows, Daggers, Staves, Sacred Seals, Claws, Whips, Colossal *, Reapers, etc.) |

## Curse considerations

| Verdict | Curses (owned text) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Near-free | Reduced Strength and Intelligence (Int free, Str mild), Reduced Intelligence and Dexterity | Int D. Dex C is minor on a halberd. Exact stat reduction size unknown. |
| Acceptable | Reduced Dexterity and Faith, Reduced Faith and Strength, Reduced Rune Acquisition, Repeated Evasions Lower Damage Negation, Reduced Damage Negation After Evading (he blocks rather than rolls) | Low impact on a guard playstyle |
| Costly | Reduced Vigor and Arcane (Vigor S), Reduced Flask HP Restoration, Reduced Damage Negation for Flask Usages, Near Death Reduces Max HP, All Resistances Down, Continuous HP Loss | Hurt the tank's HP pool or sustain |
| Below-Max-HP family | Rot / Poison Buildup When Below Max HP, Lower Attack When Below Max HP | Preferred over `Taking Damage` variants, but a tank sits below max HP often, so not free |
| Near-disqualifying | Taking Damage Causes Death / Sleep Buildup (worst), …Poison / Rot / Frost / Blood Loss / Madness Buildup | Unknown whether blocked chip damage triggers them (CLAUDE.md curse rules) |
| Disqualifying | Ultimate Art Charging Impaired | Wings of Salvation is the team's revive button |

## Meta notes and patch history

| Patch | Change |
|---|---|
| 1.02.1 (2025-08-12) | Successful-guard effects now trigger on all guard actions that should count |
| 1.02.2 (2025-09-10) | Str/Dex level-up gains up; Steel Guard angle widened; Wings of Salvation range up; **halberd guard counter AP up** |
| 1.03 (2025-12-03, Forsaken Hollows) | **Improved Guard Counters** (passive and relic) increased |
| 1.03.2 (2026-01-15) | Guardian physical and elemental negation up; Guard Boost while guarding up; **HP Recovery From Successful Guarding** up; Draw enemy attention while guarding up; **shockwave power** up |
| 1.03.5 (2026-03-31) | Hotfix only (multiplayer bug) |

Consensus: still a halberd guard-counter tank and team-revive pick. Tier lists disagree on his overall ranking, from C-tier to "best tank/support". I found no Deep-of-Night-specific Guardian guide dated after 1.03.2.
