# Scholar (Forsaken Hollows DLC)

> Researched 2026-10-30. Sources at bottom. Game patch at time of research: App 1.03.5 (multiplayer hotfix, 2026-03-31); last balance patch 1.03.2 / Regulation 1.03.4 (2026-01-15). A PS4/PS5-only update (1.23 / 1.003.004, 2026-09-18) was reported by MP1st with no gameplay changes. No newer balance patch found.
> Anything marked **(unverified)** came from a single source or conflicting sources.

## Kit

| | |
|---|---|
| Base stat scaling | Arcane S (50 from Lv1, highest in game), Vigor B, Mind B, End B, Dex C, Int C, Faith C, Str D (wiki.gg). Lv15: Vig 41, Mind 20, End 25, Str 14, Dex 18, Int 28, Fai 15, Arc 50. HP 900, FP 145, Stamina 98. Short roll (11 i-frames vs 13). |
| Starting weapon | Scholar's Thrusting Sword (Fandom, search snippet). wiki.gg notes no weapon scales with his Arcane, including the starter. |
| Passive | **Bagcraft**: about 2x consumables per slot (darts 8→12); starting relic consumables doubled. Each item category levels up with use: Lv2 stronger, Lv3 adds a bonus (e.g. darts: stacking +damage-taken debuff up to +20% for 40 s; Crystal Tears: all attributes +5 for 120 s; Boiled Crab: +30% max HP). |
| Character Skill | **Analyse**: monocle builds a progress dial on each enemy in view (decays when off-target; distance and nearby allies affect speed; new "specimens", i.e. enemy types, start higher). Below 50%: one step of enemy attack down, or self stamina up. Above 50%: double. Full on enemy: next hit does bonus stance damage. Full on self: one-hit negate shield plus a staggering shockwave. Self-use costs FP (implied by the deep relic; base cost not published). |
| Ultimate Art | **Communion**: threads enemies (and allies); invulnerable while casting. Damage to one threaded enemy deals 20% to the other threaded enemies; threaded allies share healing; enemies that hit threaded targets take damage back. Gauge cost not published. |
| Stats that matter | Arcane (status buildup, items), Vigor, Endurance. Dex only with the deep swap. |
| Stats that don't (near-free curses) | Strength, Faith. Intelligence is mostly free (C; used by some items). |

"Skill" = Analyse. "Ult/Art" = Communion. "Specimen" = a distinct enemy type you've analysed (inferred from the relic wording). "Character Skill on self" = Analyse aimed at yourself (the shield).

## Character-tagged relic effects

Ratings: **Sup** = status/consumable support, **Comm** = Communion burst, **Dex** = Dex melee.

| Effect | Kind | What it does (wiki.gg) | Sup | Comm | Dex | Owned |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Scholar] Allies targeted by Character Skill gain boosted attack | std | Allies within Analyse range: +20% physical, +15% affinity damage for 30 s | core | good | good | 6 |
| [Scholar] Prevent slowing of Character Skill progress | std | Slows decay of the Analyse dial when the target leaves view | good | niche | good | 5 |
| [Scholar] Continuous damage inflicted on targets threaded by Ultimate Art | std | 15 damage/s for 20 s per threaded target. Flat 300 total; no word on depth scaling. | niche | core | niche | 24 |
| [Scholar] Earn runes for each additional specimen acquired with Character Skill | std | 1300 runes per new enemy type analysed | good | good | good | 3 |
| [Scholar] Reduced FP consumption when using Character Skill on self | deep | FP cost x0.85 for 40 s after a self-Analyse. Unclear if it covers all FP use or only the skill **(unverified)**. | good | niche | good | 26 |
| [Scholar] Improved Endurance and Dexterity, Reduced Intelligence and Arcane | deep | End +6, Dex +40, Int −4, Arc −20 (wiki.gg) | skip | skip | core | 7 deep, 1 std |
| [Scholar] Improved Mind, Reduced Vigor | deep | Mind +9, Vigor −3 | good | good | niche | 7 deep, 1 std |

Every known [Scholar] effect is owned.

**Stat swaps:**
- **Dex +40 / Arcane −20**: this flips him from an Arcane status user to a Dex weapon user. Arcane −20 cuts status buildup (Arcane S is his whole niche), so skip it for support. Dex +40 is unusually large compared with other swaps, so confirm it in-game **(unverified)**.
- **Mind +9 / Vigor −3**: cheap. Only worth it if FP runs dry (self-Analyse spam, skills on status weapons).
- The standard copies are DLC Scene relics (Grand Luminous Scene: End +3 / Dex +3 + swap; Grand Burning Scene: Vigor +3 / Mind +3 + swap). They aren't in my unique-relic list, so query the db for their relic_ids.

## Meta notes and patch history

| Patch | Change |
|---|---|
| 1.03 (2025-12-03) | DLC launch: Scholar added |
| 1.03.1 (2025-12-17) | Communion no longer breaks when Executor transforms; Communion no longer lingers with no targets; "Communion Grants Anti-Dragon Effect" now adds physical damage; many Analyse/Bagcraft fixes |
| 1.03.2 (2026-01-15) | Analyse: adjusted distance criteria for progress against large enemies; fixed Lv3 Warming Stone not helping the user; fixed DLC costumes/unique relics disappearing |

- Consensus: B tier (nightreignwiki.com 2026 and two Steam Deep of Night posts). Valued as support, not damage.
- Guides agree he's a support: Analyse debuffs, status weapons, shared consumables. Weapon picks vary (Antspur Rapier, Frozen Needle, Reduvia, Rivers of Blood).
- Gaps: base FP cost of Analyse and the Communion gauge cost aren't published anywhere I found.
